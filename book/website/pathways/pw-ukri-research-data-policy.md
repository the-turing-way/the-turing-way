# UKRI Research Data Policy

:::{curation} UKRI Research Data Policy
---
depth: 2
description: >
  The UKRI Research Data Policy sets out expectations for managing and sharing research data and research-relevant digital objects, including code, models, software and artificial intelligence artefacts, arising from UKRI-funded research.
  These curated chapters support researchers to plan data management, prepare a data management plan, document data and metadata, share data responsibly so it is as open as possible and as closed as necessary, choose licences and repositories, assign persistent identifiers, write data access statements, acknowledge contributors, and preserve data in line with the FAIR principles.
  This pathway is a learning resource and does not confirm compliance; always consult the official UKRI policy, its annexes, your award terms and your institution's research data service.
